### B. Phương trình bậc hai: tóm tắt (học Thứ 2)

**ax² + bx + c = 0 (a ≠ 0)**

| Δ = b² − 4ac | Kết luận |
|---|---|
| Δ > 0 | 2 nghiệm phân biệt: x₁,₂ = (−b ± √Δ) / 2a |
| Δ = 0 | Nghiệm kép: x₁ = x₂ = −b / 2a |
| Δ < 0 | Vô nghiệm |

**Công thức thu gọn** (khi b chẵn, đặt b = 2b'): Δ' = b'² − ac; nghiệm x₁,₂ = (−b' ± √Δ') / a.

**Trường hợp đặc biệt (không cần Δ):**
- c = 0: ax² + bx = 0 → x(ax + b) = 0.
- b = 0: ax² + c = 0 → x² = −c/a.
- **a + b + c = 0** → x₁ = 1, x₂ = c/a.
- **a − b + c = 0** → x₁ = −1, x₂ = −c/a.

### C. Bài kiểm tra cuối tháng 10 (làm Thứ 7, 30 phút)

**Phần 1: Chia động từ trong ngoặc**

1. By the time we got to the station, the train ________ (leave).
2. She ________ (live) in this city since 2015.
3. I'll phone you as soon as I ________ (finish) my homework.
4. Look! The children ________ (play) in the rain.
5. Neither the teacher nor the students ________ (be) ready yet.
6. The number of cars in the city ________ (increase) every year.
7. Every boy and girl in the class ________ (have) a uniform.
8. Mathematics ________ (be) my favourite subject.

**Phần 2: Viết lại câu sao cho nghĩa không đổi**

9. People say that he is a talented singer.
   → He ________________________________________________
10. They will build a new bridge next year.
   → A new bridge ______________________________________
11. I had a mechanic repair my bike.
   → I had my bike _____________________________________
12. They made him clean the room.
   → He ____________________________________________________
13. People believe that the thief escaped through the window.
   → The thief _________________________________________

**Phần 3: Điền make hoặc do (chia đúng dạng)**

14. You should ________ an effort to improve your English.
15. Could you ________ me a favour, please?
16. Don't worry. Everyone ________ mistakes.
