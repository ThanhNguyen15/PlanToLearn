## Tài liệu

### A. Từ vựng *Population & Gender Equality* (20 từ khởi đầu)

| Từ/cụm | Nghĩa | Từ/cụm | Nghĩa |
|---|---|---|---|
| population growth | sự gia tăng dân số | birth rate / death rate | tỉ lệ sinh / tỉ lệ tử |
| ageing population | dân số già hóa | life expectancy | tuổi thọ trung bình |
| overpopulation | quá tải dân số | densely populated | đông dân |
| gender equality | bình đẳng giới | gender discrimination | phân biệt giới tính |
| stereotype | khuôn mẫu, định kiến | prejudice | thành kiến |
| equal opportunities | cơ hội bình đẳng | equal pay | trả lương bình đẳng |
| empower | trao quyền (empowerment) | eliminate | loại bỏ (elimination) |
| role | vai trò | domestic | thuộc gia đình (domestic chores) |
| breadwinner | trụ cột kinh tế | workforce | lực lượng lao động |
| treat sb equally | đối xử bình đẳng | be biased **against** | có thành kiến với |

### B. Bài kiểm tra cuối tháng 1 (30 phút)

**Phần 1: Viết lại câu dùng đảo ngữ, bắt đầu bằng từ cho sẵn**

1. He had hardly arrived when it started to rain. → Hardly ________________________
2. She not only sings well but also dances beautifully. → Not only ________________________
3. I only realized my mistake when I got home. → Only when ________________________
4. I have never seen such a beautiful sunset. → Never ________________________
5. As soon as he left, the phone rang. → No sooner ________________________

**Phần 2: Câu giả định**

6. I wish I ________ (know) the answer now.
7. I wish I ________ (not eat) so much last night.
8. It's time we ________ (go) home.
9. I'd rather you ________ (not tell) anyone about this.
10. The teacher suggested that he ________ (revise) more carefully.

**Phần 3: So sánh và modal + have V3**

11. The more you practise, ________ (good) you become.
12. This is ________ (interesting) book I've ever read.
13. It's getting ________ (cold) as winter comes.
14. The ground is wet. It ________ (must / rain) last night.
15. You ________ (should / tell) me earlier! I could have helped you.
16. He ________ (can't / steal) the money; he was with me all evening.

<details>
<summary><b>Đáp án (chỉ mở sau khi làm xong)</b></summary>

1. Hardly had he arrived when it started to rain.
2. Not only does she sing well, but she also dances beautifully.
3. Only when I got home did I realize my mistake.
4. Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.
5. No sooner had he left than the phone rang.
6. knew
7. hadn't eaten
8. went
9. didn't tell
10. (should) revise
11. the better
12. the most interesting
13. colder and colder
14. must have rained
15. should have told
16. can't have stolen

**14–16:** rất tốt · **11–13:** khá · **dưới 11:** ôn lại tuần 14–16 trong tuần 19.
