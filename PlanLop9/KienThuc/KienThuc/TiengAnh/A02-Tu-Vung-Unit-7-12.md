## Kiểm tra nhanh

1. Phong Nha – Ke Bang has many ________ (hang động).
2. We booked a ________ (tour trọn gói) to Da Nang.
3. She speaks English ________ (trôi chảy).
4. Many animal species are ________ (có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng).
5. We should use ________ (tái tạo được) energy such as solar power.
6. This laptop is light and ________ (dễ mang theo).
7. He has the right ________ (bằng cấp) for the job.
8. Being a doctor is a ________ but ________ job. (vất vả – đáng làm)
