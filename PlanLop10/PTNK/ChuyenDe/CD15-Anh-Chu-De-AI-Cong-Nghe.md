## Vì sao cần chuyên đề này?

- Đề tiếng Anh Sở TP.HCM 2026 dùng nhiều **chủ đề thời sự**: **AI, deepfake, robot dọn nhà, metro, đồng hồ thông minh, nền tảng nghe nhạc trực tuyến**. Bài đọc hiểu về **deepfake** có tính phân loại vì đòi hỏi **suy luận**.

- Đề chuyên Anh (Sở và PTNK) thường có bài đọc, bài luận về **Technology & Society**.

> Chủ đề này lặp lại ở **cả 4 bài thi tiếng Anh** (Anh chung Sở, chuyên Anh Sở, Anh chung PTNK, chuyên Anh PTNK) và còn hỗ trợ **bài NLXH** môn Văn ([CĐ14](CD14-Van-Thoi-Dai-AI-Cam-Xuc-Con-Nguoi.md)).

## 2. Word formation theo họ từ (dạng câu 29 – 34 đề Sở, phần Writing đề PTNK)

| Động từ | Danh từ | Tính từ | Trạng từ / phủ định |
|---|---|---|---|
| automate | automation | automatic | automatically |
| rely | reliance, reliability | reliable / reliant | **un**reliable |
| innovate | innovation, innovator | innovative | innovatively |
| verify | verification | verifiable | **un**verifiable |
| — | authenticity | authentic | **in**authentic |
| manipulate | manipulation | manipulative | — |
| addict | addiction, addict | addictive / addicted | — |
| access | access, accessibility | accessible | **in**accessible |
| replace | replacement | replaceable | **ir**replaceable |
| — | ethics | ethical | **un**ethical, ethically |
| respond | response, responsibility | responsible | **ir**responsible |
| secure | security | secure | **in**secure |
| digitize / digitalize | digitalization | digital | digitally |
| — | intelligence | intelligent | intelligently, **un**intelligent |
| depend | dependence | dependent | **in**dependent |

> **Bẫy hay gặp:** *addictive* (gây nghiện – tính chất của vật) ≠ *addicted* (bị nghiện – trạng thái của người). *reliable* (đáng tin) ≠ *reliant on* (phụ thuộc vào).

**Bài 1 – Dạng đúng của từ trong ngoặc**

1. Many students are worried about the ______ of their personal data. (*private*)
2. Human creativity is ______; no machine can fully copy it. (*replace*)
3. Information from unknown websites is often ______. (*rely*)
4. Smart homes make daily tasks more ______. (*automate*)
5. Using AI to cheat in exams is clearly ______. (*ethics*)
6. Short videos can be highly ______, so set a time limit. (*addict*)
7. The new app is ______ to people with poor eyesight. (*access*)
8. Always look for ______ of a viral story before sharing it. (*verify*)

<details>
<summary><b>Đáp án bài 1</b></summary>

1. privacy · 2. irreplaceable · 3. unreliable · 4. automatic · 5. unethical · 6. addictive · 7. accessible · 8. verification

</details>

## 4. Đọc hiểu (mô phỏng mức đề Sở, 8 phút)

> **AI Study Buddies: Helpful or Harmful?**
>
> More and more teenagers are using AI chatbots to help with their homework. These tools can explain difficult ideas in simple words, create practice questions and give feedback on essays within seconds. For students who are too shy to ask questions in class, a chatbot can feel like a patient private tutor.
>
> However, teachers warn that the same tools can become a shortcut. When students copy answers without thinking, they may finish their homework faster but learn very little. Another problem is accuracy. AI chatbots sometimes produce information that sounds convincing but is completely wrong, so students who do not check facts may repeat these mistakes.
>
> Experts suggest a simple rule: think first, ask later. Students should try a problem on their own for at least fifteen minutes before asking a chatbot for a hint, not the full answer. They should also compare the chatbot's explanation with their textbook. Used in this way, AI does not replace learning; it supports it.

1. What is the passage mainly about?
   A. How to build an AI chatbot  B. The benefits and risks of using AI for studying  C. Why teachers dislike technology  D. The history of AI
2. According to paragraph 1, why may shy students like chatbots?
3. The word "**convincing**" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to ______.
   A. believable  B. boring  C. confusing  D. expensive
4. Which is NOT mentioned as a problem? A. copying answers B. wrong information C. high cost D. learning very little
5. What does "**it**" in the last sentence refer to?

<details>
<summary><b>Đáp án bài đọc</b></summary>

1. B · 2. Because a chatbot can feel like a patient private tutor (they do not have to ask in class). · 3. A · 4. C · 5. learning

</details>

## 5. Viết cụm từ theo thông tin cho sẵn (dạng mới từ đề Sở 2026)

## 6. Viết lại câu (chủ đề công nghệ)

1. People say that AI will change the job market. → **It** ______
2. I regret spending so much time on social media. → **I wish** ______
3. If you don't check the source, you may share fake news. → **Unless** ______
4. The app was so useful that millions of people downloaded it. → **It was such** ______
5. "Don't share your password with anyone," the teacher said to us. → **The teacher warned** ______
6. Students rarely realise how much personal data apps collect. → **Rarely** ______

<details>
<summary><b>Đáp án</b></summary>

1. It is said that AI will change the job market.
2. I wish I hadn't spent so much time on social media.
3. Unless you check the source, you may share fake news.
4. It was such a useful app that millions of people downloaded it.
5. The teacher warned us not to share our password(s) with anyone.
6. Rarely do students realise how much personal data apps collect.

</details>

## 8. Chuyên Anh: đề luận và ý chính (250 – 350 từ)

| Đề | Dạng | Ý chính gợi ý |
|---|---|---|
| *Some people believe AI tools make students lazy. To what extent do you agree?* | Opinion | Shortcut vs. personalised learning · depends on **how** it is used · rules: think first, verify |
| *Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of smart devices for teenagers.* | Discussion | Safety, health tracking, learning apps ↔ addiction, sleep, privacy |
| *Deepfakes are becoming harder to detect. What problems do they cause and what can be done?* | Problem – solution | Scams, reputation, trust ↔ digital literacy lessons, labelling AI content, laws |

**Cụm học thuật dùng được ngay:** *a growing body of evidence suggests that…* · *this is a double-edged sword* · *it would be naive to assume that…* · *while it is true that…, it is equally important to…* · *strike a balance between… and…*

## 9. Lỗi hay gặp

| Lỗi | Sửa |
|---|---|
| *an information, many datas* | *a piece of information*, *data* (không đếm được trong văn phong phổ thông) |
| *depend in, rely in* | *depend **on**, rely **on*** |
| *addicted with* | *addicted **to*** |
| *advices about technology* | *advice* (không đếm được) |
| Nhầm *technique / technology* | *technique* = kỹ thuật, cách làm; *technology* = công nghệ |
