# Chuyên đề 9: Viết lại câu nâng cao (sentence transformation)

> [Danh mục chuyên đề](Danh-Muc-Chuyen-De.md) · Mức ★★ · Dùng cho cả đề Anh chuyên Lê Hồng Phong và PTNK · Luyện **5 câu/ngày** từ [tuần 14](../../LHP/Tuan-14-Ke-Hoach-Hoc-Tap.md) · Thời lượng: 3 buổi học + luyện hằng ngày

## Mục tiêu

- Nhận ra ngay **cấu trúc được kiểm tra** trong mỗi câu.
- Viết lại **giữ nguyên nghĩa, đúng thì**, dùng đúng từ cho sẵn (nếu có).

---

## 1. 25 cặp cấu trúc hay gặp

| # | Câu gốc | Viết lại |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I haven't seen him for 5 years. | The last time I saw him was 5 years ago. |
| 2 | He started learning English 3 years ago. | He has been learning English for 3 years. |
| 3 | She is too young to drive. | She is not old enough to drive. |
| 4 | Although it rained, we went out. | Despite / In spite of the rain, we went out. |
| 5 | It was such a hard test that... | The test was so hard that... |
| 6 | I regret not studying harder. | I wish I had studied harder. |
| 7 | You should see a doctor. | If I were you, I would see a doctor. |
| 8 | People say that he is rich. | He is said to be rich. / It is said that he is rich. |
| 9 | Someone stole my bike. | I had my bike stolen. |
| 10 | If you don't hurry, you'll be late. | Unless you hurry, you'll be late. |
| 11 | As soon as he arrived, it rained. | No sooner had he arrived than it rained. / Hardly had he arrived when it rained. |
| 12 | I only understood when he explained. | Only when he explained did I understand. |
| 13 | He didn't realise it until later. | It was not until later that he realised it. / Not until later did he realise it. |
| 14 | It isn't necessary for you to come. | You needn't come. / You don't have to come. |
| 15 | I'm sure he didn't take it. | He can't have taken it. |
| 16 | It was a mistake to tell her. | I shouldn't have told her. |
| 17 | "Don't touch it," he said to us. | He warned us not to touch it. |
| 18 | "You broke it," she said to Tom. | She accused Tom of breaking it. |
| 19 | I prefer reading to watching TV. | I would rather read than watch TV. |
| 20 | It takes me 2 hours to do it. | I spend 2 hours doing it. |
| 21 | Prices have increased sharply. | There has been a sharp increase in prices. |
| 22 | She was tired, but she kept working. | Tired as she was, she kept working. |
| 23 | I've never seen such a nice place. | This is the nicest place I have ever seen. |
| 24 | He couldn't come because he was ill. | His illness prevented him from coming. |
| 25 | We didn't go out because it rained. | Had it not rained, we would have gone out. |

## 2. Quy trình 4 bước

1. **Đọc câu gốc**, xác định thì và nghĩa (khẳng định / phủ định, thật / không thật).
2. **Nhìn từ cho sẵn / phần đầu câu mới** → đoán cấu trúc (bảng trên).
3. **Viết**, giữ đúng thì (hoặc lùi thì nếu là câu tường thuật, điều kiện).
4. **Đọc lại so với câu gốc**: nghĩa có đổi không? Thiếu "not"? Sai thì?

## 3. Bài tự luyện (30 câu, dùng từ in hoa, không đổi từ đó)

1. I haven't seen my uncle for ten years. **LAST**
2. It's ages since I last went swimming. **BEEN**
3. He started working here in 2020. **FOR** (giả sử bây giờ là 2027)
4. The box was too heavy for me to lift. **ENOUGH**
5. Although the weather was bad, the match went ahead. **DESPITE**
6. It was such a boring film that we left early. **SO**
7. I regret not taking his advice. **WISH**
8. You should apologise to her. **WERE**
9. People believe the painting is very valuable. **BELIEVED**
10. A mechanic repaired my car yesterday. **HAD**
11. If you don't hurry, you'll miss the bus. **UNLESS**
12. As soon as he got home, the phone rang. **SOONER**
13. I only understood the problem when the teacher explained it. **ONLY**
14. He didn't realise his mistake until later. **UNTIL**
15. It isn't necessary for you to bring any food. **NEED**
16. I'm sure she didn't see us. **HAVE**
17. Perhaps she missed the train. **MIGHT**
18. It was a mistake to lend him money. **SHOULDN'T**
19. "Don't go near the edge," the guide said to the children. **WARNED**
20. "I'll carry your bag," he said to me. **OFFERED**
21. "You cheated in the test," the teacher said to Nam. **ACCUSED**
22. "Let's go to the beach," said Lan. **SUGGESTED**
23. I prefer staying at home to going out. **RATHER**
24. It took me three hours to finish the report. **SPENT**
25. The number of tourists has risen dramatically. **RISE**
26. She was very tired, but she finished the race. **AS**
27. No student in the class is taller than Minh. **TALLEST**
28. I have never read such an interesting book. **EVER**
29. He couldn't attend the meeting because he was ill. **PREVENTED**
30. We didn't go out because the weather was bad. **HAD**

<summary><b>Đáp án</b></summary>

1. The **last** time I saw my uncle was ten years ago.
2. I haven't **been** swimming for ages.
3. He has worked / has been working here **for** seven years.
4. The box wasn't light **enough** for me to lift. / I wasn't strong **enough** to lift the box.
5. **Despite** the bad weather, the match went ahead.
6. The film was **so** boring that we left early.
7. I **wish** I had taken his advice.
8. If I **were** you, I would apologise to her.
9. The painting is **believed** to be very valuable.
10. I **had** my car repaired (by a mechanic) yesterday.
11. **Unless** you hurry, you'll miss the bus.
12. No **sooner** had he got home than the phone rang.
13. **Only** when the teacher explained the problem did I understand it.
14. It was not **until** later that he realised his mistake.
15. You **needn't** bring any food. / You don't **need** to bring any food.
16. She can't **have** seen us.
17. She **might** have missed the train.
18. I **shouldn't** have lent him money.
19. The guide **warned** the children not to go near the edge.
20. He **offered** to carry my bag.
21. The teacher **accused** Nam of cheating in the test.
22. Lan **suggested** going to the beach.
23. I would **rather** stay at home than go out.
24. I **spent** three hours finishing the report.
25. There has been a dramatic **rise** in the number of tourists.
26. Tired **as** she was, she finished the race.
27. Minh is the **tallest** student in the class.
28. This is the most interesting book I have **ever** read.
29. His illness **prevented** him from attending the meeting.
30. **Had** the weather not been bad, we would have gone out.

## 4. Lỗi hay gặp

| Lỗi | Ví dụ sai → đúng |
|---|---|
| Quên đổi khẳng định ↔ phủ định trong câu điều kiện | *If I had time, I couldn't help* → *If I had time, I **could** help* |
| Đảo ngữ nhưng quên trợ động từ | *Only when he explained I understood* → *... **did I understand*** |
| Sai thì sau *wish* | *I wish I took* (cho quá khứ) → *I wish I **had taken*** |
| Sai dạng sau giới từ | *prevented him from attend* → *from **attending*** |
| Đổi từ cho sẵn | Từ in hoa phải giữ **nguyên dạng** (theo yêu cầu đề) |
