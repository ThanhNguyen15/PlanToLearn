| 1 | important | essential, vital, crucial, significant | 16 | think | believe, consider, reckon |
| 2 | big | huge, enormous, vast, considerable | 17 | try | attempt, make an effort |
| 3 | small | tiny, minor, slight | 18 | show | demonstrate, indicate, reveal |
| 4 | good | beneficial, positive, advantageous | 19 | increase | rise, grow, go up, soar |
| 5 | bad | harmful, detrimental, negative | 20 | decrease | fall, decline, drop, go down |
| 6 | difficult | challenging, demanding, tough | 21 | cause | lead to, result in, bring about |
| 7 | easy | simple, effortless, straightforward | 22 | because of | due to, owing to, as a result of |
| 8 | many | numerous, a large number of, plenty of | 23 | although | even though, despite the fact that |
| 9 | very | extremely, highly, remarkably | 24 | also | moreover, furthermore, in addition |
| 10 | help | assist, support, aid | 25 | but | however, nevertheless, yet |
| 11 | get | obtain, acquire, gain | 26 | so | therefore, consequently, as a result |
| 12 | use | utilize, make use of, employ | 27 | for example | for instance, such as, a case in point is |
