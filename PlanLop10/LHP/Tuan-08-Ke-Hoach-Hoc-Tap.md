## Tài liệu

### A. Từ vựng *Success & Failure* (20 từ khởi đầu)

| Từ/cụm | Nghĩa | Từ/cụm | Nghĩa |
|---|---|---|---|
| achieve a goal | đạt mục tiêu | set a goal | đặt mục tiêu |
| accomplish | hoàn thành (accomplishment) | attain | đạt được |
| persevere | kiên trì (perseverance) | persistent | bền bỉ (persistence) |
| setback | trở ngại, thất bại tạm thời | obstacle | chướng ngại |
| overcome | vượt qua | give up | bỏ cuộc |
| fail | thất bại (failure) | succeed **in** V-ing | thành công |
| ambition | hoài bão | potential | tiềm năng (reach your potential) |
| effort | nỗ lực | pay off | được đền đáp (Hard work pays off.) |
| learn from mistakes | học từ sai lầm | stand out | nổi bật |
| keep going | tiếp tục cố gắng | against all odds | bất chấp mọi khó khăn |

### B. Word formation – tiền tố

| Tiền tố | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| un- | không | unhappy, unable, unemployed, unlikely |
| in- | không | incorrect, independent, inexpensive, informal |
| im- (trước p, m, b) | không | impossible, impolite, immature, imbalance |
| il- (trước l) | không | illegal, illiterate, illogical |
| ir- (trước r) | không | irregular, irresponsible, irrelevant |
| dis- | không, ngược lại | dishonest, disagree, disappear, dislike |
| mis- | sai | misunderstand, misuse, mislead |
| non- | không | non-smoker, non-stop, non-profit |
| over- / under- | quá mức / dưới mức | overweight, overestimate / underpaid, underestimate |
| re- | lại | rewrite, rebuild, renewable |
| pre- / post- | trước / sau | preview, prehistoric / postwar |
| inter- | giữa, liên | international, interact |
| co- | cùng | cooperate, co-worker |

> **Mẹo:** câu có nghĩa **phủ định, chê bai**, hoặc có từ *but, although, however* trước chỗ trống → cân nhắc thêm tiền tố phủ định.

### C. Bài kiểm tra cuối tháng 11 (30 phút)

**Phần 1: Câu điều kiện**

1. If I ________ (be) you, I would apologize to her.
2. If she had left earlier, she ________ (not miss) the train.
3. ________ I known about the problem, I would have helped you.
4. You can borrow my bike ________ you return it by Friday. (as long as / unless)
5. Viết lại: *I don't have enough money, so I can't buy that laptop.*
   → If ________________________________________________
6. Viết lại: *But for his advice, I would have failed.*
   → If it ______________________________________________

**Phần 2: Câu tường thuật**

7. "I am tired," she said. → She said ______________________________
8. "Where do you live?" he asked me. → He asked me _______________________
9. "Don't touch the wire," the electrician said to us. → The electrician warned us ________________
10. "I'm sorry I broke your vase," Tom said to Mary. → Tom apologized to Mary ________________
11. "Let's go to the cinema," said Lan. → Lan suggested ______________________

**Phần 3: Word formation**

12. Her ________ (DECIDE) to study abroad surprised everyone.
13. The film was so ________ (BORE) that I fell asleep.
14. It's ________ (POSSIBLE) to finish all this work in ten minutes. Nobody could do it.
15. We need to ________ (STRONG) the relationship between the two schools.
16. He answered the interviewer's questions very ________ (CONFIDENCE).

<summary><b>Đáp án (chỉ mở sau khi làm xong)</b></summary>

1. were
2. wouldn't have missed
3. Had
4. as long as
5. If I had enough money, I could buy that laptop.
6. If it hadn't been for his advice, I would have failed.
7. She said (that) she was tired.
8. He asked me where I lived.
9. The electrician warned us not to touch the wire.
10. Tom apologized to Mary for breaking her vase.
11. Lan suggested going to the cinema.
12. decision
13. boring
14. impossible
15. strengthen
16. confidently

**14–16:** rất tốt · **11–13:** khá · **dưới 11:** tháng 12 dành thêm 2 buổi ôn phần này.
